Saalon se internet par hume ek dream becha jaa raha tha. College jaana band karo, degree waste of time hai! Bas ek twelve-week bootcamp join karo, React seekho, aur Google me two-hundred-thousand dollars ki salary paao. Lekin self-taught developers ka ye golden era ab ek wall se takra raha hai. Post-2023 market me entry-level applications ki flood aayi hui hai. Tech layoffs ki wajah se hazaaron experienced software engineers ab fir se jobs dhoond rahe hain. To kya sach me Computer Science degree ab ek hard requirement ban chuki hai? Ya aap abhi bhi ek strong GitHub portfolio ke dum par entry le sakte hain? Aaj hum fact-check karenge ultimate developer myth ko. Stop guessing, start knowing.

Chalo sach baat karte hain. Computer Science degree sirf code likhna seekhne ke baare me kabhi nahi thi. University ki real power hai iska institutional cheat code. Campus career fairs, corporate partnerships, aur direct hiring pipelines. Zyadatar top-tier tech companies cold applications ko dekhti bhi nahi hain. Unke Applicant Tracking Systems, ya ATS, degree filters ke sath pre-configured hote hain. Bina us college ki degree ke, ho sakta hai aapka resume kisi recruiter ke samne tak na pahuche. Aur fir aati hai computer science fundamentals ki baat. Bootcamps aapko sirf ek quick web app banana seekhate hain. Lekin universities aapko computer architecture, operating systems, aur discrete math ke concepts deep me seekhati hain. Ye deep theory hi aapko adaptable banati hai jab technology aur frameworks badalte hain.

Lekin college ka ek dark side bhi hai: iski bohot zyada fees aur outdated syllabus. Java 8 ya theoretical Turing machines seekhne ke liye lakhon rupaye kharch karna, aur wo bhi ek aise professor se jisne pichle twenty years se production code ship nahi kiya—ye sach me ek bohot bada scam lagta hai. Dusri taraf, industry ke best engineers sirf ek cheez ki parwah karte hain: Proof of Work. Kya aap sach me software build kar sakte hain? Ek self-taught developer jisne teen real-world products banaye hain, active open-source contribution kiya hai, aur jiska GitHub profile strong hai, wo kisi normal CS graduate se kahin zyada valuable hai. Bootcamps aur self-learning platforms directly un tools par focus karte hain jinhe companies aaj hire kar rahi hain. Wo theory ko bypass karke direct building par jaate hain, aur startups me hamesha speed ki jeet hoti hai.

Aur ab, baat karte hain AI ki. Cursor, Copilot aur LLMs ki madad se code syntax likhna ab bohot easy ho gaya hai. Koi bhi ek simple prompt likh kar working script generate kar sakta hai. Lekin core value isme hai ki pure architecture aur systems aapas me connect kaise hote hain. Isliye ab technical interviews badal rahe hain. Log boilerplate code likhne ki jagah system design, security, aur complex debugging par zyada sawal puch rahe hain. Aur ye wahi areas hain jahan CS graduates ko naturally upper hand milta hai.

To final verdict kya hai? Claim: You need a Computer Science degree to get a tech job. Ye statement hai partially true, aur hum isko rate karenge seven out of ten. Agar aapke paas time aur money hai, to CS degree ek cheat code hai jo aapko HR filters bypass karne aur direct internship dilane me madad karegi. Lekin agar aap self-taught path par hain, to aapko undeniable proof of work build karna hoga. Complex apps banao, microservices handle karo, aur direct networks build karo.
